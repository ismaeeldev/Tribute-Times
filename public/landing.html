    How It Works

    Create

    1. ✅ Enter your special date
    2. ✅ Enter the recipient's name
    3. ✅ Enter your personal message
    4. ⏳ Click Create. This can take up to 60 seconds while our system searches the internet for your date.

    Check and buy

    1. ✅ Preview your newspaper. A screen image appears, protected with a security overlay.
    2. ✅ Happy with it? Continue to payment.
    3. 💳 Make your payment. Use a discount code if you have one.
    4. ✅ Download your high-resolution PDF.

    Print and give

    1. 🖨️ Print it at home, as often as you like. It's yours! We recommend high-quality paper and a simple frame from your local print shop.
    2. ❤️ Present it to the recipient and wait for the smile. That's your reward for being so thoughtful 😇
    3. 🎁 Come back and leave us a review to receive a second discount offer.
    4. ❤️ Who's next? Who else would you like to put a smile on today?
